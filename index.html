SellerScope Global

SellerScope Global is a Chrome extension and website at getsellerscope.com. It shows sales estimates, a margin calculation and competition scores beside the marketplace product page a seller already has open. Estimates are modelled ranges. They are a research aid, not a seller’s actual orders or a promise of profit.

Who operates it

SellerScope is operated by Sarvadhi Digital Solutions Pvt Ltd, Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India. Support is support@getsellerscope.com. SellerScope is not affiliated with Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra or Meesho.

What it does

The current release is version 6.4.2, dated 22 October 2026. The research interface is English, with en-IN number formatting. There is no Hindi language selector. Review analysis can read English, Hindi and Hinglish review text. That is separate from translating the interface.

  • Sales estimates: monthly unit and revenue ranges from rank and category, in the currency of the page. These are model outputs, not seller-reported sales.
  • Margin calculator: referral, weight-handling and closing fees for the page in front of the user. Indian fees include GST. US prices are before state sales tax. Canadian fees include a 5% GST estimate on Amazon fees.
  • Observed offers counted separately from identified sellers. Unknown fulfilment stays unknown. A Buy Box-only capture does not establish the total number of offers or sellers.
  • Version 6.4.2 adds cross-platform store and brand lookup on Flipkart, Myntra and Meesho from the observed product brand and byline.
  • Account-owned saved research lists, notes and CSV export. Saved earlier and later observations are selected endpoints, not continuous monitoring.

Marketplaces

Amazon India, Amazon.com, Amazon.co.uk, Amazon.ca and Amazon.ae, plus Flipkart. Cross-platform store lookup also opens searches on Myntra and Meesho. Capabilities and data availability vary by marketplace. The Amazon.in search table stays off until its release flag is on.

Plans

Prices are in Indian rupees. Annual prices are the full year, not a monthly installment. Unlimited plan lookups stay subject to endpoint rate limits and data availability. Workspace quotas apply after account sign-in. The free key created on install cannot open a workspace.

What it does not claim

  • Sales and revenue figures are not actual orders, settlement reports or guaranteed profit.
  • Search results and partial seller or store samples are not the whole marketplace or complete inventory.
  • The research dashboard, account watches and alert inbox in the 6.5 candidate are not in the current release.
  • Fulvia catalog and order connections are paused and unavailable.
  • SellerScope does not sell user data. It reads the public marketplace page the user already has open.
