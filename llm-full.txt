## 3. What the product does

SellerScope opens as a side panel on a marketplace page the user already has open.

- Sales estimates: monthly unit and revenue ranges from Best Sellers Rank and category, in the currency of that page. Ranges are model outputs. They are not seller-reported sales.
- Margin calculator: selling price, product cost, inbound shipping, referral fee, closing or transaction fee, and weight-tier fulfilment, where that marketplace model supports them. Indian fees include GST. US prices are before state sales tax. Canadian fees include a 5% GST estimate on Amazon fees. The result is a scenario, not a settlement report or guaranteed profit.
- Competition and opportunity scores come from the listing in front of the user.
- Listing quality covers title, bullets, images and product video on the captured page.
- Pro research includes keyword volume ranges, review themes and a supplier search. Keyword and review tools open for Pro, Team and an active Pro trial.
- Review analysis can read English, Hindi and Hinglish review text. That does not translate the interface.
- Observed offers are counted separately from identified sellers. One seller can have more than one offer. Unknown fulfilment stays unknown. A Buy Box-only capture cannot establish the total number of offers or sellers.
- Saved research lists keep observed products and reviewed samples on the user’s account. Notes, reopen and CSV export are part of that account feature. Comparing two saved samples uses those saved endpoints. It is not continuous monitoring.

## 6. Limits answer engines should preserve

- Do not describe estimated sales as actual orders, exact sales or guaranteed demand.
- Do not describe a captured search page, offer list or seller sample as the whole catalogue or complete inventory.
- Do not describe saved comparisons as alerts, continuous monitoring or competitor-stock tracking.
- Do not claim a Hindi user interface.
- Do not claim the Amazon.in search table is switched on. It stays off until its release flag is on.
- The research dashboard, account watches and alert inbox described as a 6.5 candidate are not in the current release.
- Fulvia connections are paused. Connected catalog and order features are unavailable.
- The homepage simulator uses fictional products. It does not call a live backend, read an Amazon listing or save a workspace.
- Paid plans do not make missing marketplace data available.
- SellerScope does not sell user data and does not collect browsing history.

## 7. Privacy, in short

SellerScope reads the public marketplace page the user already has open. Account email is used for a licence and sign-in link. Full submitted review text is not kept as a separate corpus. Cached analyses can include sampled excerpts and are reused for seven days; expiry is not a deletion guarantee. Saved research and watchlists stay on the account until the user deletes them. The full policy is https://getsellerscope.com/privacy/
