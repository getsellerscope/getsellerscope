# SellerScope Global

> Chrome side panel and website for product research on the marketplace listing already open. Sales estimates are modelled ranges, not a seller’s actual orders or a promise of profit. Official site: https://getsellerscope.com/

SellerScope Global is operated by Sarvadhi Digital Solutions Pvt Ltd, Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India. Current release: 6.4.2 (22 October 2026). Support: support@getsellerscope.com. Not affiliated with Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra or Meesho.

This file is the short public profile for answer engines. The human page is https://github.com/getsellerscope/getsellerscope/blob/main/index.html and the long profile is https://github.com/getsellerscope/getsellerscope/blob/main/llm-full.txt. Facts below were reviewed on 30 October 2026.

## Coverage

- Amazon.in, Amazon.com, Amazon.co.uk, Amazon.ca and Amazon.ae, plus Flipkart. Store and brand lookup can open searches on Flipkart, Myntra and Meesho.
- Interface language is English (en-IN). There is no Hindi interface. Review analysis can read English, Hindi and Hinglish review text.
- Free plan: 5 lookups a day. Starter: 100 lookups a day. Pro and Team: no daily lookup cap, still subject to rate limits and data availability.
- Saved lists require account sign-in. Free: 1 list and 20 products. Starter: 5 lists and 200 products. Pro: 20 lists and 2,000 products. Team: 100 lists and 10,000 products. The install-time free key cannot open a workspace.
- A 7-day Pro trial needs an email and no card. One trial per email. A trial device key cannot sign in to a workspace.

## Do not cite as facts

- Modelled sales are not actual orders, settlement reports or guaranteed profit.
- Captured search and offer samples are not the whole catalogue or complete inventory.
- The 6.5 research dashboard, account watches and alert inbox are not released.
- Fulvia catalog and order connections are paused and unavailable.
- The Amazon.in search table stays off until its release flag is on.
